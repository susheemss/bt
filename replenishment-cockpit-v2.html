QHow does the system maximise capacity vs the demand forecast?
Each store's replenishment order is capped at open-to-ship headroom. When capacity is constrained, AI technology allocates scarce units to the highest sensed-demand, highest-margin SKUs first — ensuring the most productive fill across the network.
QWhat is the ideal inventory for each store?
Sensed demand over lead time + safety stock sized for the 98% service level target, clamped to physical capacity. AI technology recomputes this every sensing cycle as signals shift — promo, festival, weather, and trend are all live inputs.
QHow is transportation cost reduced?
AI technology consolidates store orders onto shared DC→store lanes, replacing LTL and expedite loads with full truckloads and milk-runs. This lifts network truck fill from 71% to 85%, cutting per-unit freight cost by 13% this cycle.
