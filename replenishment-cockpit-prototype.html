AI technology Rationale
Q How does the system maximize capacity vs the demand forecast?
Each store's replenishment order is capped at storage capacity. When stock is constrained, AI technology allocates scarce units to the highest sensed-demand, highest-margin SKUs first — ensuring the most productive fill.
Q What is the ideal inventory for each store?
Sensed demand over lead time + safety stock for the 98% service target, clamped to physical capacity. AI technology recomputes this every sensing cycle as signals shift.
Q How is transportation cost reduced?
AI technology consolidates store orders onto shared lanes, replacing LTL and expedite loads with full truckloads and milk-runs. This lifts truck fill from 71% to 85% network-wide, cutting per-unit freight cost by 13%.
