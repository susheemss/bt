AI technology Decision rationale
QHow does the plan stay within each store's capacity?
Each order is capped at the physical space available in the store. When space is tight, higher-demand SKUs are prioritised so every shelf metre is used productively.
QHow much stock should each store hold?
Enough to cover sales during the 2-day delivery window, plus a safety buffer to maintain 98% service level. This is recalculated each week as demand signals — promo, weather, events — change.
QHow is freight cost being cut this week?
By combining smaller store orders onto shared trucks, average truck fill has gone from 71% to 85%. That brings the per-unit freight cost down by 13% — a saving of £1,300 compared to dispatching each store separately.
